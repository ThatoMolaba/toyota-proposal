09 · What could go wrong

Six things that could go wrong. And what happens when they do.

If…What happensWho acts
A question isn’t covered by approved contentAllsight doesn’t guess. It tells the person someone will follow up, hands the question over and flags the gap.The right Toyota team answers. Toyota approves any new content.
A translation reads wrongFirst-language speakers review key answers in the set-up week. Anyone can ask for a person at any point.Allsight corrects. Toyota signs off.
A sensitive matter is mentioned in passingRouting rules lean towards a person. If in doubt, Allsight hands over with the full conversation.HR or employee relations, in confidence
There’s breaking news or a crisisAllsight uses only the holding statement Toyota approves. Every comment request goes to the spokesperson.Corporate Affairs and communications
Personal information is involvedPOPIA opt-in, a clear STOP option and data kept in Toyota’s systems. Workforce insight only shows groups above an agreed size.Allsight runs the controls. Toyota sets the rules.

15 · Questions you may have

The questions we expect. Short answers.

Does Toyota have to replace any systems?No. Allsight works on top of what Toyota already runs. Hand-overs reach the right teams where they already work.
Who decides what Allsight says?Toyota. Allsight answers only from Toyota-approved content, and nothing new goes out without approval.
What if Allsight doesn’t know the answer?It doesn’t guess. It hands over to a person with the full conversation and flags the gap for approval.
Can workforce insight be traced to a person?No. Dashboards show anonymous, aggregated figures above an agreed group size. Sensitive matters never appear.
Are WhatsApp and Meta charges extra?No. They are included in every tier, and every other channel is unlimited.
Which languages does it cover?All 11 official spoken languages, from the same approved content. Key answers are checked by first-language speakers.
What if Toyota stops after the month?Toyota keeps the conversation data, the insight report and the response playbook.
Who is Allsight?A subsidiary of Stems Corp (Pty) Ltd, a South African company. Live at Jetour and Chery dealerships through Motus, among others.
